    Record, stay silent for about 1 second, read the whole passage, stay silent for about 1 second, stop. The small numbers (S1, S2 ...) are for the plan only: do not read them aloud.

    Reject and retake if any of these is true
    1. A word is missing, added, repeated or badly mispronounced.
    2. The meter went red or the waveform has flat tops (clipping).
    3. A cough, door, phone or chair noise is in the take, especially in or next to the flawed part.
    4. The flaw was weaker than planned, in the wrong place, or mixed with another flaw (for example getting louder while going faster).
    5. A false start, or the take was interrupted.
    6. Something in the setup changed (headset moved, volume touched).

    At most 3 attempts per take, then take a break. Never reject a take because of what the software does with it.
